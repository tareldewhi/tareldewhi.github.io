  <h3 class="occurrence-title">THE CORRELATED CITY</h3>

  <p class="occurrence-meta">
    The Primary Event · <span>INTERFERENCE</span>
  </p>

  <p class="occurrence-text">
    The Correlated City. The Annihilated City. The Burned City.
    The Chrysalis City. The New City.
  </p>

  <p class="occurrence-quote">
    Then, somewhere down there, something in the dying city suddenly crumbled,
    with a weary gasp followed by an intermittent gurgle that shook Telli's body
    like a leaf and left her speechless.<br>
    It was then that Meros began to understand the language of the dead.
    To understand everything the vocabulary of the living could never fully describe.
    <br><br>
    “We live a suspended life,” Telli confirmed through a flood of tears.<br>
    “Does that seem fair to you?!” she said in a strangled voice, forcing out every word,
    no longer able to hold back the underground river of anger that ran
    through the ruined city. Choked by that emotion, she moved to strike him;
    and he would have let her, but all that came of it was an awkward,
    fierce embrace that seemed never to end.
    <br><br>
    The intimacy Meros and Telli had failed to experience when they had found themselves
    in bed together, they finally experienced now.<br>
    It tasted of asphalt, because both of them carried the entire weight of their people on their backs.
  </p>
